# Climbing Safety: Moab Area

## Overview
Moab, Utah is one of the premier climbing destinations in North America, offering world-class crack climbing, sport routes, and big wall objectives. The primary areas are Indian Creek, Potash Road (Wall Street), and the cliffs of Castle Valley.

## Potash Road (Wall Street)
Located on Utah Highway 279, less than 5 minutes from downtown Moab. One of the most accessible crags in the area with 441 total climbs:
- 325 trad routes, 99 sport, 17 boulder problems
- Difficulty range: 5.4 TR to 5.12+, with the sweet spot at 5.10–5.11
- Key sectors: Wall Street (150 routes), Long Canyon (146 routes), Day Canyon (55 routes)
- Many moderate classics in the 5.8–5.9 range make this ideal for visiting intermediate climbers

**Critical sandstone rule:** Do not climb during or immediately after rain. Wet sandstone is permanently damaged by climbing — the soft surface tears under friction. Check the forecast; wait at least 24–48 hours after precipitation before climbing sandstone.

## Indian Creek
Located 50 miles south of Moab in Bears Ears National Monument. Indian Creek is the world's premier destination for hand-crack climbing. The parallel sandstone walls produce perfect, consistent cracks from finger to offwidth width.

Characteristics: Routes are typically single-pitch, 60–100 feet, requiring large gear racks (many climbers bring 3–4 sets of cams in the .75"–3" range for a single pitch). Be aware of raptor nesting closures — the Cat Wall and Reservoir Wall have seasonal restrictions posted at the trailhead.

## Rope Systems and Gear
For Moab sandstone trad climbing:
- A 70m dry-treated rope (9.5–10.2mm) is standard
- Large rack: doubles or triples in hand-to-fist sizes (.75"–3") are often needed
- Wear a helmet — rockfall is a constant hazard, especially on popular routes with traffic above

## Belay and Anchor Safety
- Use a locking carabiner on your belay device, clipped to the belay loop only
- Build anchors using SERENE-A: Solid, Efficient, Redundant, Equalized, No Extension, Angle under 60°
- Assisted-braking devices (Grigri) are popular but do not eliminate the need for an active brake hand

## Communication and Emergency
Moab has spotty cell coverage. File a trip plan with a trusted person. The nearest hospital with trauma capability is Moab Regional Hospital (719 W 400 N, Moab). For emergencies in the backcountry: 911 may not connect — a satellite communicator (Garmin inReach, SPOT) is strongly recommended for remote crags.

## Seasonal Conditions
- **Spring (March–May)** and **Fall (Sept–Nov)**: Best seasons. Mild temps, stable weather.
- **Summer**: Extreme heat. Early morning starts only; off the wall by noon.
- **Winter**: Cold but often climbable on south-facing walls. Watch for ice on approaches.
