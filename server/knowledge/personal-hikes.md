# Personal Hike Reviews — Moab & Surrounding Area

These are firsthand reviews of trails personally completed in and around Moab, Utah.

---

## Slickrock Trail
- **Location:** Moab, Utah (Sand Flats Recreation Area)
- **Difficulty:** Easy
- **Best For:** Getting a feel for slickrock terrain, mountain biking crossover trail
- **Personal Notes:** A fun, accessible trail on the famous sandstone slickrock surface that Moab is known for. Great introduction to desert hiking on exposed rock.
- **Tips:** Wear grippy shoes — the sandstone traction is surprisingly good but smooth-soled shoes are slippery. Bring more water than you think you need.
- **Season:** Year-round; avoid midday in summer heat.

---

## Corona Arch
- **Location:** Near Moab, Utah (off Hwy 279 along the Colorado River)
- **Difficulty:** Moderate
- **Distance:** ~3 miles out and back
- **Best Part:** Climbing up right next to the arch — the scale of it is incredible up close. Larger than the famous Delicate Arch.
- **Tips:** Watch for trains — the trail crosses active railroad tracks near the trailhead, so look both ways. The route involves a couple of short ladder/rope-assist sections on slickrock.
- **Season:** Spring and summer. Go early in summer to beat heat. Spring is ideal.
- **Personal Notes:** The arch itself is massive and you can walk right under and around it. Less crowded than Delicate Arch.

---

## Delicate Arch
- **Location:** Arches National Park, Moab, Utah
- **Difficulty:** Moderate
- **Distance:** ~3 miles out and back
- **Best Part:** The arch itself — the reveal at the top of the trail where it suddenly comes into view is one of the most iconic moments in all of Utah hiking.
- **Tips:** Take your time on the steep sections of the trail — there are exposed ledges near the top that require careful footing. No shade on the upper trail. Start early.
- **Season:** Spring and summer. Sunrise and sunset hikes are especially rewarding — the arch glows red in late afternoon light.
- **Personal Notes:** Worth every step. The crowds are manageable if you go early morning. The final bowl approach to the arch is unlike anything else.

## Wild Horse Slot Canyon
- **Location:** San Rafael Swell area, Utah (southeast of Price, northwest of Moab)
- **Difficulty:** Moderate
- **Best Part:** The entire canyon — the narrow walls, light beams, and sculpted sandstone make it a full sensory experience from start to finish.
- **Tips:** **Watch the weather forecast carefully — flash floods are extremely dangerous in slot canyons.** Never enter if there is rain in the forecast anywhere in the watershed, even miles away. Check weather for the entire region, not just the trailhead.
- **Season:** Spring and summer. Avoid monsoon season (July–September) due to flash flood risk.
- **Personal Notes:** One of the most beautiful slot canyons in the region. The colors and texture of the walls are incredible. A must-do if you can go on a clear weather day.
